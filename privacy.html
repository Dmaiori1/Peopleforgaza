Privacy Notice

Development-stage notice — November 2026. This site currently consists of informational pages. It has no built-in account system, donation checkout, analytics scripts, or story submission form.

Information collected

These pages do not intentionally collect personal information through forms. The hosting service, once configured, may process technical access logs such as IP addresses and browser information under its own policies. Hosting arrangements and this notice must be reviewed before launch.

Third-party fonts

The English homepage loads font stylesheets and font files from Google Fonts. Opening that page connects your browser to Google’s servers, so this is not a site with no third-party requests. The Arabic page uses system fonts. Font loading and the provider’s privacy information must be reviewed alongside the hosting arrangements before launch.

Stories and payments

No personal stories, identity documents, or payments should be submitted through this website yet. Any future submission or payment service will require an updated privacy notice describing the operator, purpose, recipients, storage, retention, deletion, and security measures.

Contact and requests

A secure contact channel has not yet been established. Do not post sensitive personal details in public GitHub issues or comments.
