Funding transparency

Every family's story deserves dignity, and every donor deserves to understand how contributions are handled.

Planning-stage policy — donations are not yet open. This describes the proposed approach, not an active payment arrangement or a promise that funds are currently being transferred.

Proposed allocation

Our starting goal is to allocate 95% of each donation to family assistance and 5% toward operating the platform, before any separately charged payment-processing, currency-conversion, or transfer fees. The final amounts reaching families will depend on the payment provider and transfer method.

Why an operating allocation?

Running a safe and reliable platform can involve website maintenance, security, payment administration, careful story review, translation, and reasonable compensation for work actually performed. The 5% is a proposed operational budget, not a claim that all expenses will always be exactly 5%.

Our commitments

  • Clearly disclose all relevant fees before a donor completes a donation.
  • Explain whether any third-party costs are deducted from, or added to, a donation.
  • Keep records separating family assistance from operating expenses and publish meaningful financial summaries once donation activity begins.
  • Never describe a donation as going 100% to families if fees or expenses reduce the amount.
  • Publish verified family stories only with informed consent and appropriate safeguards.

Before fundraising begins

We will review the applicable legal requirements, organizational structure, payment-provider terms, international transfer options, and how funds are accounted for. We may update this proposed allocation before donations launch, and we will publish the final terms clearly before accepting any funds.

For now, People for Gaza is not collecting donations through this website.
